# AI & Data Science Career Intelligence

## 📌 Project Overview

AI & Data Science Career Intelligence is an end-to-end data science project that analyzes the careers, salaries, skills, AI adoption, job satisfaction, and career decisions of professionals working in AI and data-related fields.

The project uses a dataset containing **5,000 professional records and 27 variables** to uncover patterns in career development, compensation, AI adoption, and job mobility.

The goal is to turn raw career data into **actionable insights for data professionals, employers, recruiters, and career planners**.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

The AI and data science job market is changing rapidly.

Professionals need to understand which skills, experience levels, industries, and career choices are associated with better career outcomes. At the same time, the increasing use of AI tools is changing how professionals work and how they perceive the future of their careers.

This project investigates these relationships using data analysis, SQL, visualization, machine learning, and an interactive dashboard.

### Key questions include:

- Which factors are associated with higher salaries?
- Which industries offer the highest compensation?
- How does experience affect salary?
- Which programming languages are most common?
- Does having ML in a job title relate to salary?
- How widely are AI tools being used?
- Does AI-tool usage differ across industries and experience levels?
- Which professionals are more likely to switch jobs?
- Does upskilling relate to career outcomes?
- How does fear of AI automation vary across careers?
- Does remote work relate to salary and job satisfaction?

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains **5,000 records and 27 variables** describing professionals working in AI and data-related careers.

### Main categories of variables

#### 👤 Career Profile
- Job title
- Experience level
- Years of experience
- Education level
- Primary programming language
- Certifications

#### 🏢 Work & Company
- Employment type
- Company size
- Company location
- Employee residence
- Industry
- Remote work ratio
- Team size
- People management

#### 💰 Compensation
- Salary currency
- Salary in USD
- Equity offered
- Bonus percentage

#### 🤖 AI Adoption
- ML in job title
- Daily AI-tool usage
- AI-tool hours per week
- Fear of AI automation

#### 📈 Career Behaviour
- Job satisfaction
- Interviews to offer
- Job switching
- Upskilling hours
